## Getting Started

### Accessing the Application

Connect The Dots is available at [your domain]. The application is designed to work on desktop and mobile browsers.

### Registration and Login

1. From the homepage, click "Sign Up" to create a new account
2. Enter your details:
   - Username
   - Email address
   - Password
3. Submit the form to create your account
4. After registration, log in using your email and password

## Account Management

### Viewing Your Profile

1. Click on your username in the top-right corner
2. Select "Profile" from the dropdown menu
3. Your profile page displays:
   - Your bio and personal information
   - Threads you've created
   - Your activity history

### Updating Your Profile

1. Navigate to your profile page
2. Click "Edit Profile"
3. Update your information:
   - Profile picture
   - Bio
   - Personal information
4. Save your changes

### Managing Account Settings

1. Click on your username in the top-right corner
2. Select "Settings" from the dropdown menu
3. Update your preferences:
   - Password
   - Email notifications
   - Privacy settings

## Creating and Managing Threads

### Creating a New Thread

1. Click the "+" button in the navigation bar or the "New Thread" button on the home page
2. Enter a title for your thread
3. Add tags to categorize your thread
4. Write a description for your thread
5. Click "Create" to save your thread
6. You'll be redirected to the graph editor to build your knowledge graph

### Editing a Thread

1. Navigate to the thread you want to edit
2. Click the "Edit" button
3. Make your changes to the title, tags, or description
4. Click "Save" to update the thread

### Deleting a Thread

1. Navigate to the thread you want to delete
2. Click the "Delete" button
3. Confirm the deletion

## Building Knowledge Graphs

### Graph Editor Interface

The graph editor allows you to create visual knowledge graphs with nodes and connections.

#### Adding Nodes

1. Click the "Add Node" button in the toolbar
2. Enter the node name
3. Select the node type
4. Add any additional attributes
5. Click "Create" to add the node to your graph

#### Creating Connections

1. Click on a source node
2. While holding, drag to the target node
3. Release to create a connection
4. In the popup, select the relationship type
5. Add any additional attributes
6. Click "Create" to establish the connection

#### Editing Nodes and Connections

1. Click on a node or connection to select it
2. The properties panel will show all attributes
3. Update the values as needed
4. Click "Save" to apply changes

#### Arranging the Graph

1. Click and drag nodes to reposition them
2. Use the zoom controls to adjust the view
3. Click "Auto-arrange" to automatically organize the layout

## Interacting with Content

### Viewing Threads

1. Browse the home page to see trending and recent threads
2. Click on a thread title to view its details
3. The thread view shows:
   - Thread information
   - Knowledge graph
   - Comments and discussions

### Following Threads

1. Navigate to a thread you're interested in
2. Click the "Follow" button
3. You'll receive notifications about updates to this thread

### Commenting on Threads

1. Scroll to the comments section at the bottom of the thread
2. Enter your comment in the text box
3. Click "Post" to publish your comment

### Upvoting and Downvoting

1. Click the up arrow to upvote content you find valuable
2. Click the down arrow to downvote content you disagree with
3. Your votes help determine trending and popular content

## Notifications

### Viewing Notifications

1. Click the bell icon in the navigation bar
2. The notification panel shows all your recent notifications
3. Unread notifications are highlighted

### Managing Notifications

1. Click on a notification to view the related content
2. Click "Mark all as read" to clear all notifications
3. Click the "X" on a notification to remove it

## Search Functionality

### Basic Search

1. Click the search icon in the navigation bar
2. Enter your search terms
3. Press Enter or click the search button
4. Results will include:
   - Threads matching your query
   - Users matching your query
   - Graph nodes matching your query

### Advanced Search

1. Click "Advanced Search" in the search dropdown
2. Specify filters:
   - Content type (threads, users, nodes)
   - Date range
   - Tags
   - Categories
3. Click "Search" to view filtered results

## Analytics

### Trending Content

1. The "Trending" section on the home page shows popular content
2. Trending is determined by:
   - Recent activity
   - Upvote/downvote ratio
   - Comment activity
   - Follow count
